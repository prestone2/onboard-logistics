# Editorial photo assets

The site uses 13 distinct generated editorial photographs. The current `-branded.webp` variants incorporate the supplied Onboard Logistics logo as scene-appropriate vehicle decals, workwear branding, equipment decals or building signage. They are illustrative scenes, not documentary photographs of the company’s employees or facilities.

Unbranded project originals and the original generated PNG files remain available. Branded copies are optimized WebP files; their aspect ratios are preserved.
